## Capital Vineyard Giving

Regular monthly outgoing to Capital Vineyard, typically **$100–160 NZD**, coded as automated payments (AP) with reference "Capital Vine".

- Appears most months; amount varies (commonly $100, occasionally up to $160)
- Payee nickname: Giving payee
- Separate ad-hoc POS payments to "CAPITAL VINEYARD" also occur (e.g. $40), likely for events or purchases rather than regular giving
