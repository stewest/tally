# Memory Hygiene

Reusable finance knowledge is patterns, not a second copy of the bank
statement.

**Remember:** cadence, typical amount *range*, merchant or payee *name*,
account *nickname*, how to categorise.

**Do not remember:** account numbers, card numbers, IRD/tax IDs, bank
references, or tables of individual transactions. Those stay on host-app
rows. Follow skill `BUS404`.
